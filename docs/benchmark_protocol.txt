CRAFTED thermal prediction and heat-observable protocol
Declared before pilot fitting results are inspected.

Source: corrected CRAFTED2.0.1, DOI10.5281/zenodo.10120180. GCMC data, not experiments. Primary sampling unit is source structure identifier; 12 force-field/charge variants per structure are repeated modeling conditions. Two gases are separate targets.
Training: all valid273/298K uptake points with0<P<=1bar. Test: actual323K points within the shared TRAINING FUGACITY support after Peng-Robinson conversion using source parameters. Original pressure and supplied variability columns retained. No uptake smoothing. Invalid/nonfinite/negative uptake points flagged; a group needs6points per training curve and6supportedtestpoints.
Gas fugacity and heat convention: use the source PR correction. Published enthalpy is cov(U,N)/var(N)-RT; compare predicted positive heat with minus this value. Do not addRT again. This is the source's fugacity-coordinate heat observable, not measured cycle heat.
Models: same-temperature-weighted nonlinear thermal Langmuir (existing9starts); fixed-grid nonnegative independent-site dictionary (525kernels, E0..100kJ/mol in5steps, logb298 -10..14 in1steps); fugacitywise logq versus1/T comparator. No test uptake or enthalpy enters fitting or model selection. All reported enthalpy values are held-out observables. Pressurewise baseline supplies uptake only, not an invented heat.
Loss: equal total training weight at each temperature, normalized by training maximum uptake. Supplied error values are not assumed to be standard errors or confidence intervals and are not used as inverse-variance likelihood weights. They are retained for descriptive quality audits.
Metrics: test uptake RMSE/training peak, mean absolute heat discrepancy(kJ/mol) at finite323K observations withdefinedpredictions, finiteheat coverage; per-structure summaries and force-field/charge sensitivity. Report model failures and boundary weights. Fitted saturation capacity and site distribution are not treated as independently measured or uniquely identified.
Pilot:100sourceIDs ordered by SHA256(structure_id),CO2,DREIDING/DDEC. Pilot is for execution and interpretation checks; fixed model specifications above will not be selected using test performance.
Full: allcomplete temperaturegroups for bothgases and all12variants, ifpipelinechecks pass. Results indexed bystructure/gas/forcefield/charge. Comparisons among variants must cluster bystructure; they are not32568independentmaterials.
Interpretation: gooduptake prediction does not establish heat-prediction accuracy; a point-estimate dictionary's heat error does not prove the entire independent-site family inadmissible. No material/process regeneration-energy ranking is claimed without capacity/heat-property identifiability and path completion.
