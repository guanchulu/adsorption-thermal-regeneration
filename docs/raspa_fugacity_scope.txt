DECISION
The CRAFTED pressure column is pressure, not a declaration that fugacity equals pressure. Its published workflow uses Peng-Robinson (PR). Prefer a deterministic PR conversion f_bar=phi(T,P)*P_bar before fitting/predicting the site dictionary, retaining original P for reporting. A pressure-coordinate pilot may remain as an explicitly declared low-pressure f approximately P approximation. It must not be described as exact ideal-gas source data.
Published enthalpy already contains the -RT term. Reference positive adsorption heat is Qst_reference=-Enthalpy_kJ_per_mol, with no extra RT. Never take an absolute value indiscriminately: signed positive source enthalpies should remain negative heat or be separately flagged, not silently flipped.

REPRODUCIBLE PURE-COMPONENT CONVERSION
Use R=8.314462618 J mol^-1 K^-1, P in Pa, T in K.
kappa=0.37464+1.54226*omega-0.26992*omega^2
alpha=[1+kappa*(1-sqrt(T/Tc))]^2
a=0.45724*alpha*(R*Tc)^2/Pc; b=0.07780*R*Tc/Pc
A=a*P/(R*T)^2; B=b*P/(R*T)
Solve Z^3+(B-1)Z^2+(A-3B^2-2B)Z-(AB-B^2-B^3)=0.
Select largest real vapor root Z>B for the stated273/298/323K, 0<P<=1bar conditions.
ln(phi)=Z-1-ln(Z-B)-A/(2sqrt(2)B)*ln[(Z+(1+sqrt(2))B)/(Z+(1-sqrt(2))B)]
f_bar=exp(ln(phi))*P/100000. At P=0 take the analytic limit phi=1; zero-pressure points cannot enter a log-pressure basis directly.
This conversion uses only gas properties and declared P,T, with no uptake/Qst labels. All train, held-out uptake and Qst query coordinates must use the SAME convention. Precompute the30 pressure-temperature values per gas; never apply a correction only to held-out data.

HEAT COMPARISON AND DERIVATIVE CAUTION
For the fugacity-coordinate site model, Q_f=-R*dln(f)/d(1/T)|q equals the response-weighted site enthalpy sum(w_i E_i theta_i(1-theta_i))/sum(w_i theta_i(1-theta_i)). Compare this positive heat to NEGATIVE the published DeltaH. E_i are effective fitted enthalpy parameters, not bare potential-well binding energies. Adding RT to the weighted E_i or to -DeltaH would double-count a contribution already built into the heat convention.
If using pressure coordinates, label Q_P=-R*dln(P)/d(1/T)|q as an approximate surrogate. Its relation to the fugacity derivative is
Q_P=[Q_f+R*(partial ln(phi)/partial(1/T))_P]/[1+(partial ln(phi)/partial ln(P))_T].
The R term here must use kJ/mol if Q is in kJ/mol. Do not quote the fixed-P fugacity temperature derivative alone as the total heat correction.
Example numerical finite-difference check at CO2,1bar: the numerator correction is -0.04727/-0.04121/-0.03620kJ/mol at273/298/323K, but pressure derivative terms also act. A hypothetical Q_f30kJ/mol gives Q_P30.16779/30.12408/30.09242kJ/mol. This is a derived illustrative conversion, not a fitted CRAFTED result or a bound on refitting sensitivity.

READY-TO-USE REPORTING
Preferred: Training and prediction use PR fugacity computed from archived gas critical constants while plots retain the original pressure axis. Positive reference adsorption heat is the negative of the published fluctuation enthalpy; no additional RT correction is applied. Source simulation build hashes are unavailable, while the paper-reported RASPA2.0.45 convention is verified against its official source tag.
Pilot fallback: The pressure-coordinate model uses f approximately P only over0.001-1bar. The underlying CRAFTED simulations use PR fugacity. The approximation was quantified from the declared gas constants and is explicitly separated from molecular-model validity and site-parameter identifiability.
