HOT SOURCE PROVENANCE CHECK — 2026-10-07
Scope: read-only verification of the six retained Zeolite 13X records under DOI 10.1007/s10450-020-00296-3. Only this report was written in the project. No dataset, category, uptake convention, temperature or source override was changed.

VERDICT
The public Springer supporting document confirms that Figure A.6 contains experimental symbols reproduced from Son et al. (2018), alongside model lines. The original experimental-source attribution is therefore confirmed, rather than inferred solely from the NIST exp tag. This is one reused experimental dataset for one material, not new independent experiments by Tun and Chen and not six independent sources/materials. The six NIST numeric series are visually consistent with the experimental symbol sequences, but complete point-by-point digitization certification and the exact A.6 subpanel used by the NIST digitizer remain unresolved. Absolute versus excess uptake remains UNVERIFIED and must remain unknown.

Figure A.6 has eight panels:
  a,b: Langmuir, log-log and linear;

UNITS AND TEMPERATURES
The inspected publisher figure axes explicitly use pressure inbar and amount adsorbed in mol/kg. The NIST mmol/g uptake unit is numerically equivalent to mol/kg, so no scale-factor discrepancy is indicated by these axes. This establishes units for Figure A.6, not the absolute/excess thermodynamic convention.
The original figure legends carry temperatures ending in .15K; NIST stores the six values as integerK. This0.15K metadata difference is confirmed. It was NOT silently corrected here. Any later source-adjudicated temperature correction must preserve the original database values and its provenance.

ABSOLUTE / EXCESS AND ACCESS LIMIT
All six NIST isotherm_type fields are empty. Neither the accessible Springer figure axes/caption nor the accessible ACS abstract explicitly establishes an absolute or excess uptake definition. The original Son experimental Methods, including any buoyancy/adsorbed-volume correction, were not inspected. Thus neither absolute nor excess is confirmed. Low pressure and the use of Langmuir models do not establish the convention by themselves. Retain unknown; do not fit or report it as verified absolute occupancy without additional primary-method evidence.

SOURCE DEPENDENCE / VALIDATION CONSEQUENCE
The experimental provenance is Son2018 -> Tun&Chen2021 supplementary FigureA.6 -> NIST digitization -> current six-temperature subset. Son2018 and Tun&Chen2021 belong to one experimental-source family for leakage control. A read-only search of the current39988-record inventory found no records stored directly under DOI10.1021/acs.jced.8b00019, but that absence does not turn the Tun paper into an independent experimental source. If original Son data are added later, they must be joined to this source family before train/test splitting or counting independent validation.
The six temperatures provide six conditions on one registered material, with correlated provenance and possible common experimental/digitization errors. They are not six materials, six independent sources, or six independent replications. A withheld temperature can test interpolation/generalization within that dataset; it is not independent laboratory validation or proof of a microscopic site mechanism.

RECOMMENDED WORDING
Publicly digitized CO2/zeolite13X isotherms at six temperatures, reproduced in Tun and Chen's supplementary FigureA.6 from the measurements of Son et al., were used as a source-dependent case study. The measured-symbol provenance and figure units were checked against the publisher supplement; the database's unspecified absolute/excess convention and0.15K temperature-label difference were retained explicitly.
