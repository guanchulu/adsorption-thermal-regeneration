Figure 5. Temperature transfer and an independent heat-observable check using public GCMC data. The reference cohort comprises 1,352 CO2 structure groups from CRAFTED2.0.1 under DREIDING/DDEC conditions. Structures were ordered by the thermal single-site model's held-out uptake NRMSE, with structure ID as the tie-breaker; the empirical nearest-rank rule ceil(p n) selected GERNOI at p = 0.5 (rank 676) and REYBAA at p = 0.9 (rank 1,217). (a,b) Original uptake observations (symbols) and thermal single-site curves (lines). Only uptake at 273 and 298 K enters fitting. The 323 K observations and predictions are restricted to the shared training-fugacity support, giving nine test points per selected structure. Pressure remains the reporting coordinate; Peng-Robinson fugacity is used within the model. (c) Fully withheld 323 K heat observables for the same structures: symbols are minus the reported adsorption enthalpy and lines are the constant-energy model predictions. No additional RT is added to the source convention. (d) All 1,352 reference structures, with GERNOI (blue circle) and REYBAA (orange square) highlighted. NRMSE is normalized by the maximum training uptake; heat error is the mean absolute discrepancy on the finite common heat support. The vertical axis is linear below 0.01 kJ mol^-1 and logarithmic above it, retaining the near-zero-error point. Source-supplied variability columns are retained in the source table but are not assigned confidence-interval semantics or used as inverse-variance weights. These comparisons test temperature extrapolation and the held-out enthalpy observable; they do not validate regeneration duty or identify physical saturation capacity.
