Figure 6. A literature-parameter model connects heat accounting to feasible release. The zeolite13X DSL parameters and assumed dry heat capacity come from Santori and Luberti (2016, Table8 and Eq29 text). Our single-CO2 path uses Ta298K, fa0.15bar, fd0.10bar, Td<=423K, Q5.362mol/kg and C0.858kJ/kg/K. (a) Equilibrium loading at fd; triangle: initial loading at fa. Dotted lines mark303/323K, measurement temperatures reported by the underlying equilibrium citation (Lopes etal.,2010). Dashed: >323K extrapolation;298K is also below those measurement temperatures. No segment represents experimental path validation. (b) Dry-solid, binding and retained-gas contributions. (c) Release from inverting full or reduced heat for the fixed parameterization. Reduced heat omits retained gas; its50kJ/kg target actually requires51.5339kJ/kg. The prescribed pressure step requires10.2983kJ/kg, and the423K cap produces the plateau. (d) Fixed-model release versus constructive lower and enclosed upper limits for freely chosen independent-site affinities at identical Q and C. Intervals are deterministic optimization bounds, not statistical error bars or realizable material gains. This is a model illustration with assumed C, not measured-duty validation or reproduction of the source compressor cycle.
